Since 1896

A family name built into the landscape.

Harry Rose was a bricklayer who arrived in Mistley as the maltings boom transformed the peninsula. Five generations later, the streets he and his descendants built are still the streets we build on today.

  1. 1896

    Harry Rose sets up as a bricklayer in Mistley, building for the maltings trade.

  2. 1920s

    H L Rose & Sons builds the ribbon streets of Mistley, Manningtree and Lawford.

  3. 1995

    Rose Builders Ltd is incorporated — the modern contractor takes shape.

  4. 2019

    The next generation strengthens the board as the new-homes arm grows.

  5. 2025

    A record year: £70.5m turnover, 192 people, and an HBF 5-star award.
